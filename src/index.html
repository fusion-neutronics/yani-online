Irradiation schedule

Step Flux (n/cm²/s) Duration

A cooldown's duration is the length of that step, not a cumulative time, so log-spaced sampling means differencing the times you want. A scenario's pulses run at the selected preset's full-power flux, or else at the first pulse's. Five years at 80% availability is five 0.8 a pulses with 0.2 a breaks, then cooling to 100 a.
